DeeFoodie Privacy

Effective 2026-09-15 · Published by Capricorn Systems, Karachi, Pakistan

Summary

DeeFoodie is a private journal of where you eat in Karachi. We don't have analytics, ads or tracking, and we don't sell data.

Your visits, photos and collections are stored on DeeFoodie's private server so your two devices stay in sync. Delete my data: Settings → Privacy.

What stays on your device

  • Onboarding answers and display name
  • Local visit drafts and the offline Karachi archive
  • Your API access token in the iOS Keychain
  • Photos you attach before they upload

What leaves your device

ServiceWhat is sentWhyWhen
DeeFoodie API (your private host) Visits, photos (GPS EXIF stripped), collections, bearer token Sync between your devices When you save or sync while online
Wikimedia Commons Image requests for venue or cuisine illustrations Show place and cuisine imagery When a card loads a remote image
Device location Processed on device only Near-me suggestions When you allow Location While Using

Your controls

Export your data: Profile → Journal export where available. Erase all data: Settings → Privacy → Delete my data.

Children

DeeFoodie is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over.
